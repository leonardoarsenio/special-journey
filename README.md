About the Project

Astro-Health is a health monitoring platform designed to support astronauts during space missions.

The system tracks key biometric indicators, helps identify significant changes in health conditions, and provides timely alerts to support informed decision-making. In environments where direct medical assistance may be limited by distance and communication delays, continuous health monitoring becomes essential for crew safety and mission success.

Astro-Health aims to contribute to the well-being, safety, and operational readiness of astronauts throughout their journey.

MVP Objectives

The first version of Astro-Health focuses on the following core capabilities:

Monitor essential health indicators in real time.
Display biometric data through an intuitive dashboard.
Detect values that fall outside configured thresholds.
Generate health-related alerts and notifications.
Provide access to historical health records and trends.
Enable healthcare professionals to monitor crew members remotely.

Disclaimer: Astro-Health is a monitoring and decision-support tool and is not intended to provide medical diagnoses.

The project combines technology, data visualization, and health monitoring to help improve astronaut safety and mission resilience in environments where immediate medical intervention may not be available.
